Manage your Climate Change Agreements (CCA)

Climate Change Agreements (CCA) are voluntary agreements between UK industry and the Environment Agency to improve energy efficiency and reduce carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions. CCA holders can claim the reduced rates of the Climate Change Levy (CCL) on energy they consume in facilities covered by their agreement.

Who is eligible to apply for a CCA

CCAs are available for a wide range of industry sectors from major energy-intensive processes such as the manufacture of chemicals and paper to supermarket bakeries and agricultural processes such as intensive pig and poultry farming. A full list of eligible processes is available in Appendix A of the CCA operations manual.
